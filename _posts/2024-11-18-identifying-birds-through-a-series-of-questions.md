People who know me, know that I love birds. Friends who've seen a bird message me and asking me to identify it. It's a fun exercise and I really enjoy it. Often it entails asking them a series of questions and then getting to an identity by reduction. I put those questions into a GPT. If you've seen a bird and are curious to find out what it is, give it a try. If the final answer doesn't seem like what you saw please let me know and I can try and refine it.
